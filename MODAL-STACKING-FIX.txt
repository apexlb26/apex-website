APEX solution modal stacking fix

Problem:
The capability modal was mounted inside each solution card. Cards use CSS transforms on hover, which creates a containing/stacking context for fixed descendants. That caused the modal to be positioned/clipped relative to a card and allowed sibling cards to render above it.

Fix:
- Render the open modal into document.body with React createPortal().
- Keep the overlay position: fixed at the viewport level.
- Raise modal overlay z-index to 10000 and isolate its stacking context.
- Existing modal visuals/content/interactions remain unchanged.
